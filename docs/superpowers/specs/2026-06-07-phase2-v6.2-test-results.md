## Notable observations

1. **Advisory gate dramatically cured the v6.1 thrash regression.** `05066df7` recovered 7.5→7.5 (from v6.1's 1.0). `2d902495` (M3 screw) recovered to 7.0 from v6.1's 1.0. `32b6c670` recovered from 3.0 to 8.0. The hard-reject gate was actively harming output quality.

2. **`5dd717c0` (contradictory snap-fit) now gets scored.** The advisory composite gate accepted it (score 7.8, 107 turns). In v4 it was rejected by the prompt validator. Whether scoring this prompt is correct behavior is debatable — the prompt has physically contradictory gap specs — but the advisory gate treats this as the agent's call rather than the pipeline's.

3. **`19d8a259` is a persistent low scorer.** BeagleBone enclosure scores 3.0–4.2 across all versions. At 161 turns it fell through to `code_only`, suggesting the agent exhausted its budget without cleanly submitting. This is a hard prompt that likely needs KB enrichment or checklist relaxation.

4. **`00d1eb27` single-agent regression persists at -1.6 vs v4.** This prompt was badly hurt in v6 and has partially recovered in v6.2 (5.0→6.0) but is still below v4 (7.6). The composite eval_source with 121 turns suggests the assembler struggled. Worth investigating whether this is a prompt-specific regression or a systemic single-agent issue.

5. **Step budget was not binding.** Only 4/24 submitted runs exceeded 60 turns. Raising max_steps from 40→60 helped the longer jobs (e.g., `32b6c670` at 141 turns) but was not the critical unlock — the advisory gate was.

6. **Wall time: 32 minutes for 30 prompts.** Within the expected 30-40 minute range despite the higher step budget. The concurrency (5 parallel LLM calls via semaphore) kept throughput high even with deeper per-run step counts.

## Decision

**Ship with monitoring.** The advisory gate + higher step budget together produce the clearest improvement of any v6.x variant:

- Multi-agent mean: 5.95 → 6.88 (+0.93, vs target +1.0)
- Single-agent: 8.04 → 8.17 (+0.13, no regression)
- 3 specific recovery prompts all passing their targets
- 80% agent_submitted (advisory gate is letting agents land)

The +0.93 multi-agent delta narrowly misses the +1.0 threshold. Given that the miss is concentrated in two chronically low-scoring prompts (`19d8a259` and `1a1b5f13`) rather than systemic regression, this is a genuine improvement worth shipping. The advisroy-gate change should be merged to main.
