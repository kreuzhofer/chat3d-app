# Privacy Policy

## Introduction

Chat3D ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our AI-powered 3D modeling platform.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

- Email address (used as username)
- Password (stored as encrypted hash)
- Account creation and last login timestamps

### Chat and Model Data

When you use the Service, we collect:

- Chat prompts and conversation messages
- Generated 3D model files (3MF, STEP, STL)
- Chat context names and metadata
- Model creation and modification timestamps

### Technical Data

When you interact with the Service, we automatically collect:

- IP addresses
- User agent strings (browser and device information)
- Access timestamps and referrer URLs

### Payment Data

When payment features are available, we collect:

- Payment information processed directly by Stripe
- We store only a reference to your Stripe customer ID; we do not store credit card numbers or bank details

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

- **Service Delivery:** To provide AI-powered 3D model generation and chat services
- **User Authentication:** To verify your identity and secure your account
- **Model Generation:** To process your prompts and generate 3D models
- **Service Improvement:** To analyze usage patterns and improve our platform
- **Communication:** To send account-related emails (confirmation, password reset)
- **Security:** To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access

## Data Storage and Retention

**Storage Location:** All data is stored securely in PostgreSQL databases with industry-standard encryption and access controls.

**Retention Periods:**

- Account data: Retained until account deletion
- Chat contexts and models: Retained until manually deleted by user
- Technical logs: Retained for up to 90 days for security purposes
- Email confirmation tokens: Expire after 24 hours
- Password reset tokens: Expire after 1 hour

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

- **With Your Consent:** When you explicitly authorize us to share information
- **Service Providers:** With trusted third-party services that help us operate (e.g., email delivery, hosting providers, LLM providers for model generation)
- **Legal Requirements:** When required by law, court order, or government request
- **Security:** To protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our users

## Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and browser localStorage to provide our services:

- **Authentication Tokens:** We store JWT tokens in localStorage to maintain your login session.
- **Language Preference:** We store your selected language in localStorage.
- **Cookie Consent:** We store your cookie consent choice in localStorage.
- **Third-Party Cookies:** We do not use third-party advertising or analytics cookies.

Users can disable cookies in their browser settings, but this may impact the service experience.

## Your Privacy Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

- **Access:** Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- **Correction:** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Deletion:** Request deletion of your account and associated data
- **Data Portability:** Request export of your data in a machine-readable format
- **Opt-Out:** Opt out of non-essential communications

To exercise these rights, please contact us at team@chat3d.app

## Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

- Password hashing using bcrypt
- Encrypted data transmission via HTTPS/TLS
- Secure database access controls
- Regular security audits and updates
- Account isolation to prevent unauthorized access to other users' data

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes by email or through a notice on our website. Your continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

**Email:** team@chat3d.app
