# Account and Data Deletion

## Overview

This page explains how you can delete your account and what data will be removed from Chat3D. We are committed to giving you full control over your personal information and making the deletion process transparent and straightforward.

## Export Your Data Before Deletion

Before deleting your account, we recommend downloading a copy of your data. This allows you to keep a record of your chat contexts, 3D models, and other information for your own records.

To export your data:

1. Navigate to your Profile page
2. Click on the "Download Your Data" button in the Account Actions section
3. Save the file to your device
4. The file contains all your account data, chat contexts, and generated models

## How to Delete Your Account

Follow these steps to permanently delete your account and all associated data:

1. Log in to your account
2. Navigate to your Profile page
3. Scroll to the "Account Actions" section
4. Click the "Delete Account" button
5. Review the confirmation dialog that explains what will be deleted
6. Click "Yes, Delete My Account" to confirm the deletion

## What Data Will Be Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data will be permanently removed from our systems:

- Your account information (email address, name, password, preferences)
- All chat contexts and conversation messages
- All generated 3D model files (3MF, STEP, STL)
- All uploaded files and attachments
- Payment references (Stripe customer ID, if applicable)

## Important Information

**Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.** Once deleted, your account and all associated data cannot be recovered.

Deletion takes effect immediately. You will be logged out and will not be able to access your account.

All your generated models and chat history will be removed immediately.

We cannot recover your data after deletion. Make sure to export any data you want to keep before deleting your account.

If you have any questions or concerns about account deletion, please contact our support team before proceeding.
